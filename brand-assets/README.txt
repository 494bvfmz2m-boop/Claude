The mark
  A shield carrying three chevrons that lighten as they rise: #3D7A9C at the
  base, #6FB3D0 in the middle, #A8E6FF at the top. Read down it is escalation
  (warn, mute, ban); read up it is the night lifting. The chevrons carry the
  ramp, so the mark never needs a gradient fill. Holds at 16 px; below 24 px
  drop to two chevrons.
