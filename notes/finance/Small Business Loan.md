# Small Business Loan

SBA 7(a) loan through Willamette Community Bank, closed February 2025.

- Amount: $185,000
- Term: 10 years, 8.25% variable (prime + 0.75)
- Payment: ~$2,180/month
- Use of funds: the Diedrich roaster ($68K), roastery buildout ($74K), working capital ($43K)

Covenants:
- Debt service coverage ratio of at least 1.25×, tested annually on year-end financials.
- Monthly inventory report with signed count — see [[Inventory Management]].
- Notify the bank of any new debt over $25K.

We're at 1.48× DSCR on the trailing 12 months, comfortable but not huge. The bank liked the recurring revenue from the [[Subscription Program]] and the signed wholesale accounts in [[Local Cafe Partnerships]].

Thoughts for 2026: possibly refinance if rates drop below 7%. Do not take on new equipment debt for the [[Cold Brew Launch]] — pay cash out of the [[2026 Budget]] instead. Payments are modeled in the [[Cash Flow Forecast]].
