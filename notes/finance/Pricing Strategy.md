# Pricing Strategy

Principle: price for margin on retail, price for volume on wholesale, and never compete with grocery-store coffee on price.

Retail (12 oz bags):
- [[House Blend Profile]] — $17
- [[Single Origin Lineup]] — $19 to $24 depending on green cost
- [[Seasonal Holiday Blend]] — $19

Wholesale (per lb):
- Tier 1 (10–39 lb/wk) — $11.50 blend, $14+ single origin
- Tier 2 (40+ lb/wk) — $10.75 blend
- Tier rules and delivery minimums are in [[Wholesale Fulfillment]].

Bar drinks: 12 oz latte $5.25, drip $3.25. Cost per latte is about $1.15 including milk and cup.

Review cadence: quarterly, or immediately if green coffee moves more than 20 cents a pound (see [[Green Coffee Sourcing]] and [[Supplier Contracts]]). Last increase was +$1 on retail bags in August; we lost no measurable volume. Margin assumptions feed the [[2026 Budget]] and the [[Cash Flow Forecast]].
