# Brand Guidelines

Name: Ember & Oak Coffee Roasters. Never "Ember and Oak", never just "Ember" (that's the blend).

Voice: warm, plain-spoken, a little nerdy about coffee but never snobby. We explain, we don't lecture. Short sentences. No "artisanal", no "curated".

Colors:
- Ember orange #D9622B — primary
- Charcoal #2B2B2B — text
- Oat #F2EADF — backgrounds
- Holiday variant swaps orange for cranberry #9E2B3A (see [[Seasonal Holiday Blend]])

Type: Fraunces for headlines, Inter for body text.

Logo: the oak leaf with a flame inside it. Minimum width 24 px online, 0.75 in on print. Always give it breathing room — at least the height of the leaf on every side.

Photography: natural light, real hands, real mess. No stock photos. These rules apply to the [[Instagram Content Plan]], the [[Email Newsletter]], bag labels and the banner at the [[Farmers Market Booth]]. Cafe partners can use the "Proudly serving" badge described in [[Local Cafe Partnerships]].
