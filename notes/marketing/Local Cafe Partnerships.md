What partners get: free equipment dial-in, quarterly staff training from our [[Barista Training Program]], the "Proudly serving Ember & Oak" window badge per [[Brand Guidelines]], and a shout-out in the [[Instagram Content Plan]] when they join.

What we ask: a minimum of 10 lb/week, using the [[House Blend Profile]] or a single origin as their main espresso, and paying on time. Logistics are in [[Wholesale Fulfillment]].

Pipeline: two new cafes opening in the spring, both interested. Landing them gets us to the wholesale target in [[Owner Goals 2026]].
