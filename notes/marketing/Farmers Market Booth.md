# Farmers Market Booth

Saturdays, 9 am–2 pm, April through November at the Riverside Farmers Market. Booth fee is $45/week plus 3% of sales over $1,000.

Average Saturday: $1,180 in sales — about 60% drinks (pour-over, iced coffee) and 40% retail bags. It's also our best place to sign people up for the [[Subscription Program]]; we averaged 6 new subscribers per market in September.

Setup: two-person crew, 10×10 tent, the folding brew bar, a 3-group batch brewer on the generator. Banner and menu boards follow [[Brand Guidelines]].

Lessons:
- Sampling is great for sales but blew our shrink numbers in [[Inventory Management]]. Now we use 1 oz cups and a sample log.
- Bring twice the ice you think. Every time.
- Cold brew in growlers sold out by 11 am in July — strong signal for the [[Cold Brew Launch]].

Winter plan: the indoor holiday market in December, focusing on the [[Seasonal Holiday Blend]]. Market shifts are a good training ground for new hires from the [[Barista Training Program]].
