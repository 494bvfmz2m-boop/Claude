# Email Newsletter

Platform: Mailchimp (Essentials plan, $28/mo). 2,340 subscribers, 41% open rate, 3.8% click rate — well above industry average for food & beverage.

Cadence: every other Tuesday, plus one-off launches.

Standard sections:
1. A short note from Maya about what's happening at the roastery.
2. New coffee — usually from the [[Single Origin Lineup]], with brew tips.
3. Something useful: a brew guide, a grinder recommendation, a recipe.
4. Where to find us: [[Farmers Market Booth]] dates and new wholesale cafes from [[Local Cafe Partnerships]].

Revenue: the newsletter drove 31% of online sales last quarter, mostly bag sales and [[Subscription Program]] sign-ups. Pre-orders for the [[Seasonal Holiday Blend]] go to newsletter subscribers 48 hours before the public.

Writing follows the voice in [[Brand Guidelines]]. Next experiment: segmenting the list into home brewers vs. espresso people and sending different brew tips.
