# Cold Brew Launch

Goal: ready-to-drink cold brew in 12 oz cans and half-gallon growlers by May, sold at the bar, the [[Farmers Market Booth]], and to 3–4 wholesale partners.

Recipe: 1:8 concentrate, 18 hours at fridge temp, coarse ground [[House Blend Profile]] with 20% Ethiopia Guji for brightness. Diluted 1:1 for the can.

Open questions:
- Shelf life. Without pasteurization we're limited to 14 days. Talking to a co-packer in Eugene about nitro-canning; quote expected in January.
- Licensing. The county wants a separate food processor license for canned beverages ($340/yr).
- Equipment. Two 20-gallon toddy systems (~$2,800) — proposed as a line item in the [[2026 Budget]].

Pricing target is $5.50 per can retail, $3.10 wholesale; numbers checked against [[Pricing Strategy]]. Launch campaign belongs to the [[Instagram Content Plan]] for April–May. Roasted coffee older than 21 days from [[Inventory Management]] can be used for growlers, which also cuts waste.
