# Single Origin Lineup

We keep four single origins on the shelf at any time, rotating one every 6–8 weeks. They sell for $19–24 per 12 oz bag and carry our best margin per bag (see [[Pricing Strategy]]).

Current lineup:
- Ethiopia Guji natural — blueberry, jasmine, very sweet. Our best seller this fall.
- Kenya Nyeri AA — grapefruit, black currant. Polarizing; staff love it.
- Guatemala Huehuetenango — caramel, red apple. Doubles as a blend component.
- Colombia Pink Bourbon (coming Dec) — limited, 2 bags only.

Each new coffee gets a launch post in the [[Instagram Content Plan]] and a short write-up in the [[Email Newsletter]], including the farm or washing-station story from the importer.

Single origins are roasted on Fridays per the [[Roasting Schedule]], in 6 kg batches. The [[Subscription Program]] "Explorer" tier ships whichever single origin is newest, so rotation timing has to line up with the subscription ship date.
