# House Blend Profile — "Ember"

Ember is our flagship blend and about 55% of all coffee we roast. It goes into every wholesale espresso program and is the default drip at the bar.

Recipe (by green weight): 60% Brazil Cerrado natural, 30% Colombia Huila, 10% Guatemala Huehuetenango. Components come from [[Green Coffee Sourcing]].

Roast: medium, dropped about 90 seconds after first crack, ~15% weight loss. Tasting notes on the bag: milk chocolate, toasted almond, dark cherry.

Espresso recipe we teach accounts: 18 g in, 38 g out, 28–30 seconds. This is the core of the [[Barista Training Program]].

History: we changed the recipe in May, dropping a Honduras component that kept tasting flat after two weeks. Customers barely noticed, which was the point. Any future change has to be cupped blind by three people and announced in the [[Email Newsletter]]. The bag artwork follows the warm palette in [[Brand Guidelines]].
