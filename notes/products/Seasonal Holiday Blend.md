# Seasonal Holiday Blend — "Hearthside"

Our second year doing a holiday blend. Last year we sold 640 bags between Nov 15 and Dec 31 and ran out on Dec 18, so we're planning for 900 bags this year.

Recipe: 50% Ethiopia Guji natural, 30% Colombia Huila, 20% Brazil Cerrado. Slightly darker than the [[House Blend Profile]]. Notes of spiced plum, cocoa and orange peel.

Timeline:
- Oct 15 — labels ordered (lead time in [[Inventory Management]])
- Nov 1 — pre-orders open via the [[Email Newsletter]]
- Nov 15 — launch day at the bar and the [[Farmers Market Booth]]
- Dec 20 — last ship date for Christmas delivery

Packaging uses the red-and-cream holiday variant described in [[Brand Guidelines]]. A gift box (bag + mug + tasting card) at $48 is new this year. We'll also offer a 3-month gift option through the [[Subscription Program]]. Extra roast shifts are covered by holiday temp staff from the [[Hiring Plan]].
