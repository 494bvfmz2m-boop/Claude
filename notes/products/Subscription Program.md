# Subscription Program

Launched in March. 212 active subscribers as of Nov 1, up from 140 in June. Churn is running about 6% per month, mostly after month three.

Tiers:
- Classic — 2 bags of [[House Blend Profile]] per month, $34
- Explorer — 2 different single origins per month from the [[Single Origin Lineup]], $42
- Office — 5 lb bulk, $78, billed monthly

Subscriptions ship every other Thursday. Coffee is roasted Wednesday (see [[Roasting Schedule]]) so it arrives 2–4 days off roast.

Subscription revenue is the most predictable line in the [[Cash Flow Forecast]] and was a big part of the case for the [[Small Business Loan]]. Growth ideas: a gift subscription for the holidays tied to the [[Seasonal Holiday Blend]], and a referral code pushed through the [[Email Newsletter]]. The goal in [[Owner Goals 2026]] is 400 subscribers by year end.
