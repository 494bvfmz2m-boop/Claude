# Barista Training Program

Three levels. Everyone does Level 1; Levels 2 and 3 come with a $1/hr raise each.

Level 1 — Foundations (first 2 weeks)
- Dialing in the [[House Blend Profile]] on the Linea: dose, yield, time, taste.
- Milk steaming to latte-art standard (heart and tulip).
- Opening and closing checklists, including the cleaning routine in the [[Equipment Maintenance Log]].

Level 2 — Coffee knowledge (month 2–3)
- Cupping protocol, tasting the [[Single Origin Lineup]], describing coffee to customers.
- Running a pour-over station at the [[Farmers Market Booth]].

Level 3 — Lead / wholesale trainer (month 6+)
- Training wholesale partner staff on-site, as promised in [[Local Cafe Partnerships]].
- Troubleshooting grinders and espresso machines for accounts.

Training time is paid and scheduled outside rush hours. Progress is tracked on a shared checklist; sign-off is by Jonah or a Level 3 barista. Expectations and conduct rules are in the [[Employee Handbook]].
