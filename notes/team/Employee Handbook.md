# Employee Handbook (summary)

The full handbook is a PDF everyone signs on day one. This note is the quick version.

Hours & scheduling
- Schedules are posted two weeks ahead. Swaps are fine if you find your own cover and tell the shift lead.
- Breaks: 10 minutes per 4 hours, 30-minute meal break for shifts over 6 hours (Oregon law).

Pay & benefits
- Pay every other Friday. Tips are pooled by hours worked and paid on the same check.
- After 90 days: 40 hours of paid sick leave per year, free bag of coffee per week, 50% off merch.
- Raises tied to the [[Barista Training Program]] levels.

Conduct
- We follow the voice in [[Brand Guidelines]] with customers too: warm, plain, no coffee snobbery.
- Zero tolerance for harassment. Report to Maya, or to the bookkeeper if the issue involves Maya.

Safety
- Only trained staff operate the roaster. Fire extinguisher locations are on the back of the roastery door.

New hires get this during onboarding from the [[Hiring Plan]].
