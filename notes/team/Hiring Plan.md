# Hiring Plan

Current team: 9 people (Maya — owner, Jonah — head roaster, 5 baristas, 1 wholesale/delivery lead, 1 part-time bookkeeper).

2026 hires:
1. Part-time production roaster (Q1) — 20 hrs/week, Wednesdays and Fridays to start. Takes pressure off Jonah and lets us add a fourth day to the [[Roasting Schedule]]. $21–23/hr.
2. Online & subscriptions coordinator (Q2) — packs and ships the [[Subscription Program]], handles customer email, helps with the [[Email Newsletter]]. $19–20/hr, 30 hrs/week.
3. Holiday temp staff (Nov–Dec) — 2 people for packing the [[Seasonal Holiday Blend]] and gift boxes.

Process: post on the local job board and Instagram, 20-minute phone screen, paid 2-hour working interview, offer within a week.

All new hires go through the [[Barista Training Program]] basics, even non-bar roles — everyone should be able to pull a decent shot. Payroll costs are in the [[2026 Budget]]. Policies they sign on day one live in the [[Employee Handbook]].
