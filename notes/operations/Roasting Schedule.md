# Roasting Schedule

We roast Monday, Wednesday and Friday on the 15 kg Diedrich. Monday is the big day: wholesale orders for the week plus the [[House Blend Profile]] for our own bar. Wednesday covers the [[Subscription Program]] shipments, which have to leave by Thursday noon to arrive fresh. Friday is for small-batch [[Single Origin Lineup]] coffees and anything Farmers Market needs on Saturday.

Rules of thumb:
- First batch of the day is always a "seasoning" batch of the house blend — the drum runs cooler for the first 20 minutes.
- Log every batch (charge temp, first crack time, drop temp, weight loss) in the roast log. Target weight loss is 14–16%.
- Cupping happens Tuesday and Thursday mornings. Anything off-profile gets pulled from retail and goes to cold brew.

When wholesale volume tops 180 kg/week we'll need a fourth roast day — see [[Hiring Plan]] for the part-time roaster role. Machine downtime is tracked in the [[Equipment Maintenance Log]].
