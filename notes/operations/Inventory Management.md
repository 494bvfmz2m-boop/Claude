# Inventory Management

We track three kinds of inventory in one spreadsheet (moving to Square for Retail in Q1):

1. Green coffee — by bag and lot number, updated on arrival and every Friday after roasting. Reorder points come from [[Green Coffee Sourcing]].
2. Roasted coffee — retail bags on the shelf, wholesale stock, and the [[Subscription Program]] reserve. Roasted coffee older than 21 days is moved to cold brew or staff coffee.
3. Packaging and dry goods — bags, valves, labels, cups, oat milk. Labels have a 3-week lead time from the printer, so the [[Seasonal Holiday Blend]] labels must be ordered by Oct 15.

Monthly count happens on the last Sunday. Shrink last month was 2.1% by value, mostly from over-pouring samples at the [[Farmers Market Booth]]. Target is under 1.5%.

Inventory value feeds the balance sheet for the [[Small Business Loan]] covenant review, so the count has to be signed off by Maya before the 5th of each month.
