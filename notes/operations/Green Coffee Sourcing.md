# Green Coffee Sourcing

We buy green coffee from two importers: Pacific Bean Co. (bulk Brazil and Colombia for blends) and Altura Direct (micro-lots from Ethiopia, Kenya and Guatemala). Terms and minimums are written up in [[Supplier Contracts]].

Current positions (60 kg bags):
- Brazil Cerrado natural — 14 bags, forward-contracted through March
- Colombia Huila washed — 9 bags
- Ethiopia Guji natural — 4 bags (spot)
- Guatemala Huehuetenango — 3 bags (spot)

The C-market jumped again this quarter. Every 10 cent move per pound changes our cost of goods by roughly $1,100 a month at current volume, which is why the [[Pricing Strategy]] has a quarterly review. We aim to keep 10–12 weeks of blend components on hand and no more than 6 weeks of single origins, because micro-lots fade fast.

Arrivals get logged in [[Inventory Management]] the day they land. Samples for next season's [[Single Origin Lineup]] are due in January; cupping notes go in the shared folder.
