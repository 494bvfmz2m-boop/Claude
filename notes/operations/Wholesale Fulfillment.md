# Wholesale Fulfillment

Wholesale is 46% of revenue across 11 accounts — 7 cafes, 2 restaurants, a grocery co-op and the university catering office. Most are listed under [[Local Cafe Partnerships]].

Order flow:
- Orders close Sunday at 6 pm via the order form or email.
- Roasted Monday according to the [[Roasting Schedule]], rested overnight, packed Tuesday.
- Local deliveries Tuesday/Wednesday in the van; two accounts in Salem ship by UPS Ground.

Packaging: 5 lb bags for cafes, 2 lb for restaurants, retail 12 oz bags for the co-op shelf. Each bag gets a roast date sticker — accounts are told to use within 3 weeks.

Invoices go out the same day as delivery on Net 15 terms. Two accounts are routinely late, which squeezes the [[Cash Flow Forecast]]. Wholesale price tiers are set in [[Pricing Strategy]]; anything over 40 lb/week gets tier 2 pricing. New accounts get a free on-site dial-in session run by whoever finished the [[Barista Training Program]] lead track.
